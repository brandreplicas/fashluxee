Buying Guide: What to Look for in a First Copy

When you decide to buy celebrity replica watches, don't just go for the lowest price. Here is how to ensure quality:

Check the Movement:

Ask if it’s a "Quartz" (ticking) or "Automatic" (sweeping). For a true celebrity look, always go for Automatic.

Weight Matters:

A luxury watch should feel substantial on your wrist. If it feels like a toy, the materials are likely substandard.

Lume Quality:

High-quality copies use "Super-Luminova" so the watch glows brightly in the dark, just like the real deal.

FAQ: Everything You Need to Know

1. Can a first copy watch survive water exposure?

Most premium replicas are "Water Resistant," meaning they handle splashes and rain fine. However, we don't recommend swimming or diving with them unless specified by the seller.

2. Is the gold plating on these watches real?

While it isn't solid gold, high-end copies use "PVD Plating" which is designed to last for years without fading or turning green.

3. Why are some replicas more expensive than others?

It usually comes down to the movement and the glass. A watch with a Swiss-grade movement and Sapphire glass will always cost more but will last much longer.

4. How can I contact a seller for more live videos?

Most reputable stores provide a dedicated contact page where you can request WhatsApp videos of the watch in action before buying.
